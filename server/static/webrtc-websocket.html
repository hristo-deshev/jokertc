WebRTC signaling test (/ws)

Open this page in two tabs. Put the same session id in both, pick initiator in one and answerer in the other, then Connect in both. The initiator creates the offer; the answerer replies to it. STUN and TURN come from the relay's ready message, so a connected call also proves the credential it issues works.

Initiator and answerer are sent on the wire as the roles phone and device, which is what the relay and the device firmware speak. The relay decides who offers, and always picks the phone.

For the copy/paste version with no signaling server, see /ui/manual.
