WebRTC signaling test (/ws)

Open this page in two tabs. Put the same session id in both, pick device in one and phone in the other, then Connect in both. The relay pairs them and tells each side who offers: the phone offers, the device answers. STUN and TURN come from the relay's ready message, so a connected call also proves the credential it issues works.

For the copy/paste version with no signaling server, see /ui/manual.
